# Questions.

## What does the name mean?

РЭХА is Belarusian for “echo”, and it sounds a little like “rehearsal”.

## Is it free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license, with no account and no
subscription.

## Does it need the internet?

No. It records, plays and keeps its history with no internet in the room. It
asks GitHub which version is the latest when it starts and once a day, and
sends nothing else. It downloads a new version only when you press Update.
Settings › Under the hood switches the asking off.

## Which audio interfaces work?

Any your computer sees. On Windows choose ASIO if your interface has an ASIO
driver: the other drivers often show only two inputs.

## Is there a metronome?

Yes. Each song keeps its tempo and time signature: typed, tapped, or counted
from an old take. The click plays through the output you choose, so it can
go to the drummer's headphones only, and during a take the edge of the screen
lights on every click. A take recorded to the click keeps its tempo, and its
WAV and .mid files carry it: set your DAW to that tempo, and the take's bars
sit on the project's bars.

## Does it show where we sped up?

Yes. Under the tracks, the Master row draws the band's tempo bar by bar:
against the click the take was recorded to, or the song's tempo, or, for a
song without one, the take's own middle. Where the band ran ahead or dragged
by more than 2% (you can change that in Settings), the line turns amber and
says by how much.

## Can it play a backing track?

Yes. A song can keep one audio file, mono or stereo: a backing track, pads, a
loop. Add it on the song's page in History: the app counts its tempo and
finds bar 1, or you drag bar 1 to where you hear it. It comes in on bar 1,
right after the count-in, through the output you choose: the speakers, say,
while the click stays in the drummer's headphones. The take keeps it as a
track of its own, so you can listen to the band without it.

## Where do the recordings go?

Into RehearsalRecordings in your home folder: a folder for each rehearsal, a
folder for each take, a WAV file for each musician. Settings › Folders picks
another place.

## What if the laptop dies in the middle of a take?

The take is written to disk as it records and made safe every 30 seconds, so
you lose at most the last 30 seconds. The next launch offers it back.

## Will the laptop fall asleep in the middle of a take?

No: while a take records, РЭХА keeps the laptop and its screen awake.
Closing the lid still puts it to sleep, and the take ends there, with
everything before it kept. On battery, the recording screen asks for the
charger at 20%.

## How does the band get the takes?

Pick the good ones, or switch on automatic sending, and they are copied to a
folder your Google Drive or Dropbox app syncs, as WAV, FLAC or MP3.
