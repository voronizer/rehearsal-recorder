# Start a take without going to the laptop.

Viktor Baikov built a Bluetooth controller for РЭХА: five buttons, a knob and
a small screen. Whoever has it starts and stops takes from where they play.

- REC starts a take, and pressed again keeps it.
- PLAY plays the take back, and the knob sets how loud.
- Hold SONG or TAKE and turn the knob to pick another one.

## fine print

You build it yourself: the parts, the wiring and the firmware are on GitHub.
It works with this controller only, and so far it has been tried on a Mac.
Pick it in Settings › Controls.
