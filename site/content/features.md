# What it does.

<!-- Each tile has a piece of the app beside its words, chosen by the id in
     site/src/page/pieces.tsx. Keep the ids and their order. -->

## Shows trouble while you play. {#health}

Low disk space, a low battery, an input that clips: the recording screen says
so during the take.

## Every musician on their own track. {#track}

Each track is its own WAV file, written to disk as you play. If the laptop
dies, you lose at most the last 30 seconds.

## Notes too, from an e-kit or a keyboard. {#midi}

A track records its audio, its MIDI or both. The notes are saved as a .mid
file beside the audio.

## Every rehearsal in History. {#rehearsals}

Which songs were played, how long it ran, how much disk it takes.

## Marks say what happened. {#marks}

Keep this, Went wrong or the band's own, with a comment, right on the take.

## Two spellings, one song. {#names}

Typed Palyn at one rehearsal and Pałyn at the next? Merge them once, and the old
spelling still finds the song.

## Send takes to the band. {#cloud}

The ones you pick, or every take you save. They are copied to a folder that
Google Drive or Dropbox syncs.

## Rehearse the set, in order. {#sets}

Make the gig's songs a set once. Every take is the song you are on until you
move on, and History says which ones you never got to.

## A click for the drummer. {#metronome}

Each song keeps its tempo and time signature. The click can go to the
drummer's headphones only, and the beat flashes on screen.

## Deleting goes to the Trash. {#trash}

So does a take as it was before a crop. On Windows it is the Recycle Bin.
For 10 seconds, Undo brings it back.
