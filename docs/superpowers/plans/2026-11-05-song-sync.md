# Songs kept the same in РЭХА and the band service: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A song has one number in РЭХА and the band service; title, tempo and signature change on either side, a take moves to another song in the service, and РЭХА says who changed what.

**Architecture:** The Worker gets song numbers in its РЭХА routes (link, make, change, merge, list), who-changed columns, a band PATCH for title and tempo, a take move, and **Delete song** always to the Trash. РЭХА keeps each song's number and what both sides last agreed on, sends its own changes at once, and at each check takes the rest. The service pages get the title and tempo in place and ⋯ › **Move to song**; РЭХА gets lines in its background work and a notice.

**Tech Stack:** Cloudflare Worker (Hono + zod-openapi, Drizzle on D1, R2), React + TanStack on РЭХА's shadcn components (`service/src`, `ui/src`), Python with SQLAlchemy and Alembic.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-song-sync-design.md` (Alex's «ок» 5 November 13:43; 13:44 «делай сразу»: built at once, native).

## Global Constraints

- Titles: compared with `fold(t) = t.trim().toUpperCase().toLowerCase()` with ς→σ and ß→ss in the service (matches Python's `casefold` on the band's alphabets), `casefold()` in РЭХА. `^Take \d+$` is no song's title, as `store/names.py` has it.
- Tempo: a whole number, 30-300, 60-600 in 6/8; signatures “4/4”, “3/4”, “2/4”, “6/8”; tempo and signature both or neither.
- `MIN_REHA_VERSION = "0.23.1"`.
- Words as the spec and mockups give them; curly quotes round titles in messages; British spelling; test labels in Python cp1252 only.
- Migrations only add.
- While building, run only the tests a change touches, once each; the full suites run once, on CI.

## Review Focus

1. A ★ in РЭХА before the check, on a take the band moved in the service: the take stays moved (Task 1 test).
2. A rename in the service, then a ★ from РЭХА with no song fields: the take keeps the new name (Task 1 test).
3. The first check with РЭХА's Polyn (old name Palyn) when the service has both Polyn and an empty Palyn: one song, the files and notes of both (Task 1 test).
4. A song renamed in РЭХА with no internet to a title the service gave another song meanwhile: put back in РЭХА with its line saying why (Task 3 test).
5. A song deleted in the service while this computer holds an unsent take of it: it stays in РЭХА (Task 3 test).

**Interfaces (produced):**
- Schema: `song.signature`, `song.titleBy`, `song.meterBy`, `song.madeBy` (text, user id or null = РЭХА); `take.movedBy` (user id or null).
- `fold(title)`, `same(a, b)` by `fold`, `TAKE_NAME = /^Take \d+$/` in `shared/names.ts`.
- `TakeMeta`: `song_id?: int | null` (by number), `song?: string | null` (by title, made if none), `name?: string` (required when either is there); `tempo` gone. PUT answers `{ ok, in_trash, drift_gen, song_id }`. Without `song_id`/`song` the take keeps its song and name; with one, `movedBy` clears.
- `TakeRow.moved: boolean`.
- РЭХА routes (`needKey`): `POST /api/reha/songs/link {songs: {title, old_names, tempo, signature}[]} → {ids: number[]}`; `POST /api/reha/songs {title, tempo, signature} → {id}`; `PATCH /api/reha/songs/{id} {title?, tempo?, signature?} → {ok}` or 409 `{error, song: {id, title}}`, 404; `POST /api/reha/songs/{id}/merge {into} → {ok}`; `GET /api/reha/songs → {songs: {id, title, tempo, signature, trashed, title_by, meter_by, made_by, trashed_by}[], moved: {uid, song_id, by}[]}` (`*_by` shown names).
- Band: `PATCH /api/songs/{id}` adds `title`, `tempo` + `signature`; `PATCH /api/takes/{uid} {song} | {new_song}`; `DELETE /api/songs/{id}` always to the Trash.
- Store (`songs/sync.ts`): `findSong(db, title)`, `makeSong(db, title, meter, by)`, `renameSong(db, id, title, by)`, `setMeter(db, id, meter, by)`, `mergeSong(env, from, into)`, `linkSongs(env, list)`.
